Step-by-Step Pairing

3 Effortless Ways to Connect Any Two Devices

METHOD 1 • INVITE LINK

Share Direct Invite Link

Best when connecting with someone over WhatsApp, Telegram, Email, or remote networks.

1 Tap Copy on the Invite Link card in the My Link modal.
2 Send link to your peer. When opened, they enter their name.
3 Approve with 1 tap on your real-time notification alert!
METHOD 2 • QR SCANNER

Scan Peer QR Code

Best when pairing your phone with your desktop or two devices side-by-side.

1 On Device A, open My Link to display the high-def QR code.
2 On Device B, tap center Scan to open the HD camera.
3 Point camera at QR code for instant pairing request.
METHOD 3 • 5-DIGIT PIN

Enter 5-Digit PIN

Fastest office or lab pairing without cameras or link copying.

1 Note your peer's permanent 5-digit PIN (e.g. 48291).
2 Open My Link and enter the PIN in the pairing box.
3 Tap Send Req to initiate direct session.
Advanced Privacy Controls

Revoking Permission & Mutual Stealth Blocking

P2P Share gives you absolute control over your connection channel even after you have accepted a peer.

Revoking Permission (Lock Channel)

Tap the Lock icon in the active chat header to immediately revoke permission. The channel locks instantly, preventing any further messages or file transfers until you re-enable it.

Mutual Stealth Blocking

Tap the Ban icon to block any user. Your device immediately stops broadcasting presence to them and removes you from their active directory. You can unblock them anytime from the Blocked Users page.

Practical Scenarios

Real-World Usage Examples

Example 1 • Phone to PC

Transferring Photos from Phone to PC

1. Open P2P Share on your PC and click My Link.
2. On your phone, tap the center Scan button to scan the PC's QR code.
3. Accept on PC, then stream photos instantly with auto-save!

Example 2 • Remote Client

Sharing Code ZIP with a Remote Client

1. Copy your Invite Link and send it to your client via WhatsApp.
2. Accept their incoming real-time request alert.
3. Drop your .zip archive directly into the chat stream.

Example 3 • Office LAN Hub

Office Pairing via 5-Digit PIN

1. Share your 5-digit PIN (e.g. 59204) with your teammate.
2. Teammate types the PIN in the My Link modal and clicks Send Req.
3. Pin their contact for permanent 1-click access.
